# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want SaveWiser to be a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of age, experience level, gender identity and expression, disability,
ethnicity, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or any other
characteristic.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps create a positive environment:

- Being kind, respectful, and patient with others.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project.
- Showing empathy toward other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private.
- Trolling, personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported
to the project team at **savewiserhelps@gmail.com**. All complaints will be reviewed
and investigated promptly and fairly, and reporters' privacy will be respected.

Project maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith
may face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by the project's
leadership.
